Image 1 is our firehouse: its red roll-up slat bay door is the design to paint, in exactly this style.
Paint a flat texture of that red roll-up fire-station bay door, seen perfectly straight-on and filling the WHOLE canvas edge to edge: no arch, no wall, no frame, no side rails, no sky, no ground, no perspective. It is made of identical horizontal engine-red metal slats stacked from the very top of the image downward, TWELVE slats in the upper 90 percent, every slat exactly the same height and perfectly horizontal, running the full width with no ends visible. Each slat: a flat engine-red face (#D7262B), a thin flat cream highlight line along its top edge, one hard darker-red shadow band along its lower third, and a thick dark-brown ink seam line between slats. No rivets, dents, scratches or wear that would break the repeat; every slat identical. The BOTTOM 10 percent is the door's heavy bottom beam running the full width: a chunky brass-gold bar with a thick ink outline, one hard shadow tone and a cream highlight, with a dark rubber seal strip along its lower edge and two simple brass pull handles placed near the left and right ends (none in the middle). Flat hand-inked cel painting, no gradients, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos.
