Tall composition: the same coiled cream fire hose and brass nozzle, but the coil is now WIDE and generous: a big round coil of three chunky loops seen from slightly above fills the whole lower half of the canvas edge to edge (the coil is about 85 percent of the canvas width), and from its top the hose rises in one short thick curve into the big brass nozzle, which points diagonally up and to the right with its red bail handle, filling the upper half. The whole object is a bold, wide, heavy shape that is about 1.3 to 1.4 times taller than wide, never a thin stick.
