Paint this card picture, the FIVE-ALARM call: the same closer, lower camera looking slightly up at the top of the bay door arch of Image 2, but now FIVE of the brass alarm bells of Image 3 stand in a row along the top of the arch, every one of them RINGING HARD at once: bells tilted mid-swing, hammers blurred, bold hydrant-yellow motion arcs and flat cream sound rings overlapping from bell to bell, and red alert lamps under the cornice flashing with flat orange light cones, a few flat gold sparks in the air. Directly below the bells and in front of the closed red slat door, framed by the arch: Chief Hamm ALONE, big and centred, half-length, both arms thrown up in a triumphant all-hands call-out, mouth wide open in a mighty joyful shout, his brass bugle raised high in one fist, the other hand a clenched cartoon fist; his face sits on the horizontal middle line of the picture. Warm lit upstairs windows either side, dusk sky just visible above the cornice. Nobody else in the picture: no dog, no other pig. The mood: the biggest call of the year, everything ringing, all hands on deck.
