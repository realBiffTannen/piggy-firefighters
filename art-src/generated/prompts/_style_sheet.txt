The reference images are reel-symbol icons from our 2D cartoon slot game; they define the STYLE and MATERIAL RENDERING ONLY. Do not copy their objects. Draw NEW items in exactly that style: a bold, thick, dark-brown ink outline around every shape; chunky, rounded, slightly exaggerated forms; flat cel shading with ONE hard shadow tone and one small cream highlight per surface; light from the top left; a small saturated palette: engine red #D7262B, brass gold #E9B23B, hydrant yellow #F5D23C, hose cream #F4E9D2, smoke blue-grey #7C8AA0, dusk navy #1E2A4A (flame orange #FF7A1A only for fire). The look is clean, hand-inked game art: not glossy, not a 3D render, no gradients, no airbrush glow, no bloom, no bevel-and-emboss filter look, no drop shadows. Genuinely transparent background with no ground shadow, no frame around the sheet and no background scenery. Every item is a separate island of pixels with wide empty transparent gaps around it so no two items touch or overlap. No text, no letters, no numbers, no symbols that look like writing, no logos, no emoji. No hard hats, no construction machinery or tools, no police imagery, no lanterns, no dragons.
