Family member GRANDMA: an elderly pig lady with a neat silver-grey hair bun on top of her head, small round brass-rimmed glasses, rosy cheeks, a kind worried-but-brave smile; a knitted hose-cream cardigan over a smoke blue-grey dress to below the knee, sensible brown slippers. Her pet cat, a plump ginger tabby with a white chest, sits on her near shoulder and clings to her with its front paws wrapped around her neck, its body resting across her shoulder and upper back, so the cat touches her the whole time (the cat does not cover her hands, her arms stay free).
