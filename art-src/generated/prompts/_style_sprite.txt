The reference images are cartoon art from our 2D slot-game family; they define the STYLE ONLY. Do not copy their objects, costumes or colours. Draw a NEW item in exactly that style: a bold, thick, dark-brown ink outline around every shape; chunky, rounded, slightly exaggerated forms; flat cel shading with ONE hard shadow tone and one small cream highlight; light from the top left; a small saturated palette: engine red #D7262B, brass gold #E9B23B, hydrant yellow #F5D23C, hose cream #F4E9D2, smoke blue-grey #7C8AA0, dusk navy #1E2A4A (flame orange #FF7A1A only for fire). The look is a clean, hand-inked game icon. It is not glossy, not a 3D render, and has no gradients, airbrush glow, bevel or border. It must read clearly at 40 pixels. Composition: a single object, centred, exactly as TALL as it is WIDE so it fills a square, filling about 90 percent of the canvas. Genuinely transparent background, no ground shadow, no frame, no background scenery. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no emoji. No hard hats, no construction machinery or tools, no police imagery, no lanterns, no dragons.
